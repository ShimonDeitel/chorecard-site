Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 21, 2026

ChoreCard is built to be private by default. The app collects essentially no personal data, runs on your device, and requires no account for you or your kids. This policy explains, plainly, what that means.

The short version

  • ChoreCard does not collect, sell, rent, or share your personal information.
  • There are no advertising networks, analytics trackers, or third-party SDKs in the app.
  • The information you enter — kids' first names, chores, points, and rewards — stays on your device.
  • No account is required to use ChoreCard, and your kids never create accounts.

Information we do not collect

ChoreCard does not request or access your camera, photos, microphone, location, contacts, health data, motion sensors, or any other device permission. We do not collect device identifiers for advertising, build behavioral profiles, or track you across other apps or websites. We do not use cookies or web beacons.

Information you enter

To make ChoreCard work, you enter ordinary household information: the first names of the kids in your family, the chores you assign, point values, completion approvals, and rewards. Kids are represented by their first name and initials only — ChoreCard has no field for photos, birthdates, addresses, or other personal details about a child. This information is stored locally on your device by the app.

Sign in with Apple

ChoreCard uses Sign in with Apple to identify the parent who owns the household on the device. This provides a stable, anonymous user identifier supplied by Apple; ChoreCard never sees or handles your Apple ID password, and we do not request your name or email through Sign in with Apple. This identifier is used only to keep your data and your one-time Pro purchase associated with you on your devices.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, ChoreCard can sync your household data privately across your own Apple devices using Apple's CloudKit. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, and is not accessible to the developer. ChoreCard also stores a minimal record of whether the one-time Pro unlock has been purchased, so that status can follow your account. If you do not use iCloud, your data simply remains on the single device. We operate no servers of our own and transmit your data to no other third party.

Purchases

The optional one-time Pro unlock is sold and processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. Apple handles the transaction; ChoreCard does not receive or store your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase information is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

ChoreCard is rated 4+ and is intended to be set up and operated by a parent or guardian. It is not directed to children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), ChoreCard does not require a child to create an account or provide personal information; a child appears in the app only as a first name and initials chosen by the parent. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help you remove it.

Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the right to access, correct, export, restrict, or erase your personal data, and to object to its processing. Because the information you enter is stored on your own device and your own iCloud account, you can exercise these rights directly: you can view and edit any entry in the app, delete your account from within Settings (which wipes local data and makes a best-effort deletion of the Pro-status record), or delete the app entirely to remove the on-device data. You may also contact the data controller below for assistance.

Your rights (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, gives you the right to know what personal information is collected, to access and delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. ChoreCard does not sell or share personal information, and there is no advertising or cross-context behavioral tracking to opt out of. You can exercise your access and deletion rights directly in the app, or contact us using the details below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

Data retention and security

Because your data lives on your device and in your personal iCloud account, you control how long it is kept — it remains until you delete it in the app, delete your account, or remove the app. Your information is protected by your device's security and your iCloud account's protections. No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure, but ChoreCard minimizes risk by keeping data on-device and avoiding third-party data flows entirely.
