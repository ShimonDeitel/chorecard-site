ChoreCard

Kids earn points for chores

Turn everyday chores into points. Parents approve what's done, and a weekly leaderboard keeps the whole family motivated.

Download on the App Store

How it works

Three simple steps, no setup headaches. Everything lives on your device.

Add kids and chores

Add each kid by name and pick chores from a built-in library, each worth a point value you can adjust.

Approve what's done

Kids tap a chore as done. It lands in your approval queue, and one swipe locks in the points.

Climb the leaderboard

A weekly leaderboard ranks the kids by points earned, and points trade in for rewards.
